QUICK START

Put on headphones, then:

  1. Open SETTINGS (grid icon) and tap CALIBRATE once, so your takes land on the beat.
  2. Set the tempo (BPM) and turn LOOP on.
  3. Tap RECORD. After the count-in, sing.
  4. Tap RECORD again to stop. The loop closes on a full bar and starts playing.
  5. Tap RECORD again to add the next layer on top.
RECORD SCREEN

BPM sets the tempo for click, count-in and loop length. CLICK switches the metronome: ON (sound), VISUAL (silent, for recording without headphones) or OFF. With ON or VISUAL the display shows the beat: SONAR rings in the middle of the meter, four beat bars on top and the corners kicking out. During the count-in four rings close in on the center and burst on the one. GAIN is the input level. COUNT-IN gives one bar of clicks before recording, or none.

BARS sets a fixed loop length: FREE, 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. With a fixed length the recording stops by itself, so you can focus on singing. It only works with LOOP on.

The arc is your input meter. The six rings inside show the layers of the loop: a box lights up where a layer has sound and flashes while it plays. The status line always says what happens next.

LOOPING AND OVERDUB

With LOOP on and BARS on FREE, a recording ends on a full bar. Stop in the first half of a bar and it cuts back to the bar line, stop in the second half and it finishes the bar. With LOOP off you record a ONE-SHOT of any length.

While a loop is selected, every RECORD adds exactly one round on top (overdub) and stops by itself. RECORD during an overdub cancels it.

  • One loop holds up to 6 layers.
  • The loop point is seamless, there is no click where the loop starts again.
  • To start a fresh loop, tap NEW LOOP in TAKES.
SING OVER A TRACK
  1. In Audiotool, bounce the part of your track you want to sing over. It lands in your library.
  2. Open TAKES, tap LIBRARY and log in if asked.
  3. MY SAMPLES lists your own loops, newest first. SEARCH looks through the public library. The BPM key limits results to your tempo.
  4. Tap the play symbol to preview, USE to load it. DREX-MB takes over its tempo and shows READY: OVER BACKING.
  5. Tap RECORD. Your layers are exactly as long as the backing loop.

The backing loop plays 3 dB lower, does not count as a layer and never goes through the FX. In EDIT the box B mutes it. It is never exported, so your vocals drop straight back onto the same spot in your track.

TAKES

Every recording is one take, no matter how many layers. Takes and their settings are saved in your browser.

  • Names are automatic: SINGLE LOOP 120 03, MULTI LOOP 120 03 or ONESHOT 03. Rename in EDIT or EXPORT.
  • Tap a row to select it, its mini wave to open EDIT, DEL to delete it.
  • Nothing is selected at start, so your first RECORD always starts a new take.
EDIT

EDIT works on one layer at a time. Boxes 1 to 6 pick the layer, B mutes the backing loop.

Timing: draw a region over the wave and drag it to fix a late or early phrase. AUTO suggests regions, SNAP or FREE sets the grid, COPY duplicates, DEL silences, RESET undoes all. 1X, 2X and 4X zoom in. One-shots also get trim handles.

VOL, S (solo), M (mute), PAN, WID (stereo width) and DEL LAYER.

EQ: 8 bands from 80 Hz to 10 kHz plus low and high cut. Drag the points. FLAT, WARM, THIN, BRIGHT and PHONE are quick starting points.

FX

After EDIT everything is about the sum: all layers together go through the FX chain. The backing loop never does.

Chain: VOCODER, SNADPAD and MULTI. Tap a device to show it, the light shows if it is on. The arrows move the shown device left or right in the chain. The button at the bottom right turns it on or off. FX settings are saved with the take.

SNADPAD MB: the original SNADPAD engine. Tap and drag on the scope to play. Double tap sets a point, tap a point to hold it, double tap it to remove it, drag to move it. RUN travels the points, GLIDE smooths the way, RATE sets the speed. REC records your movement from the next bar (press PLAY first), LEN sets its length, DRIFT lets it change a little every round, STOP ends it. The arrows on the ring switch the set. CATCH and FLUSH work in the set CRUMBS. Knobs: drag up for more, double tap to reset.

VOCODER: your voice shapes a carrier. The top row picks it: CHORD plays a synth chord, DRONE a deep single tone, NOISE gives a whisper, SAMPLE picks any sound from your Audiotool library. Tap a note on the ring to set the key. MAJ, MIN and PWR set the chord and the scale. FOLLOW makes CHORD or DRONE follow your singing in that key, without it they hold the key note. OCT switches between LOW and MID. CLARITY brings out consonants, VOICE blends your own voice back in.

MULTI: MODULATION, ROBO, GRIT, DELAY and REVERB. Tap a button to switch an effect on or off, turn its knob for the amount. Hold a button and drag it left or right to change the order, the ring shows the chain. GRIT goes from fine grain over folded edges to broken micro grains.

Export includes the whole chain in your order. On SNADPAD it includes what you recorded with REC, held points and RUN. Free playing without REC is live only.

EXPORT

What you hear is what you export. The backing loop is never included.

  1. Select a take and open EXPORT.
  2. Log in with Audiotool.
  3. Check the NAME.
  4. Pick MIX (all layers), LAYER n (one layer) or ALL LAYERS (one file each).
  5. Tap EXPORT TO LIBRARY. Loops are uploaded with their BPM.

DOWNLOAD WAV saves the same file to your device. Uploads are hidden by default in Audiotool.

SETTINGS

CALIBRATE: headphones on, four high clicks count in, then clap along to 8 clicks. Do this once per device and when you change headphones.

AUTO ENHANCE: when ON, every new recording is cleaned up and evened out automatically. OFF keeps it raw.

CLICK PITCH: tunes the metronome to a note, so you can sing in key.

THEME: switches between dark and bright.

HOME SCREEN

On iPhone in Chrome: Share, then Add to Home Screen. DREX-MB then starts full screen like an app.
